# New Netherland Hotel mockup analysis

Reference: `/Users/benjaminbreen/code/Ghosts of the Machine Age Game/new netherlands hero render mockup .jpg`

## Identification

- Primary type: late-19th-century masonry hotel exterior, treated as a static architectural game landmark.
- Primary domain: object.
- Form language: hard-surface, architectural, layered shell, repeated facade modules.
- Confidence: 0.96 that the visible macro and meso forms are sufficient for a mockup-matched first pass.

## Overall form and silhouette

- A broad rectangular street-corner block rises from a two-storey dark rusticated podium.
- The near street corner is not a sharp ninety-degree corner. It is a continuous convex quarter-round bay joining the two visible elevations.
- The brick body is wider than it is deep and visually divided into a heavy base, a multi-storey shaft, a deep upper cornice, and a steep roof/crown.
- The roof is asymmetric: a large central/front hip, a conical roof over the rounded corner turret, several front and side wall gables, and smaller lateral hips.
- The crown occupies roughly one quarter of the visible building height and is essential to the silhouette.

## Spatial relationships

- The podium is flush below the brick shaft and projects slightly beyond it.
- The rounded corner is continuous through the podium, shaft, belt courses, upper cornice, and turret base.
- The corner turret grows from the upper rounded bay rather than floating on the roof.
- The entrance portal is embedded in the main front elevation, with stairs projecting toward the street.
- Window glazing is recessed behind the masonry wall plane.
- Roof gables overlap and emerge from the steep roof field; finials sit at gable and cone peaks.

## Materials and finish

- Podium: dark warm-brown sandstone/granite, dielectric, roughness approximately 0.82-0.94, deep block relief and darker joints.
- Shaft and gables: medium dark red-orange brick, dielectric, roughness approximately 0.76-0.88, subtle value variation.
- Courses and archivolts: warm brown stone, roughness approximately 0.72-0.86.
- Roof: blue-charcoal slate, roughness approximately 0.72-0.84.
- Glass: dark desaturated blue-gray, roughness approximately 0.2-0.35, restrained environment response.
- Sashes/iron: near-black, moderately rough.

The supplied textures contain baked shading and are evidence/temporary color sources, not final independent PBR channels.

3. Deep central arched entrance with projecting stairs.
4. Two-storey dark rusticated base.
