# Reference suitability

Verdict: conditional pass.

The overhead game image clearly establishes the target blocks, current gaps,
road centerlines, and the importance of the corner silhouettes. It cannot
support facade or ornament reconstruction by itself. Period photographs from
the Library of Congress, Cornell, and NYPL supply the missing landmark-level
evidence. Together these references are sufficient for a compressed low-poly
district pass. Unseen elevations and exact dimensions remain approximate.
