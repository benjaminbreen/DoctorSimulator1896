# 1896 Fifth Avenue street dressing

This pass adds three national flags and a restrained set of street and
building signs. It is atmosphere, not a claim that every individual object is
documented at its exact game coordinate.

## National flags

The game date is August 3, 1896. The 45-star United States flag became official
on July 4, 1896, following Utah's admission. The game texture therefore uses
45 stars in six staggered rows of eight and seven.

- Source: Smithsonian, [Flag facts](https://www.si.edu/es/spotlight/flag-day/flag-facts?page=1).
- Placement status: inferred. Flags appear only at the New Netherland, Savoy,
  and Metropolitan Club, rather than on every prominent building.
- Restraint cue: the many flags visible on the Windsor Hotel in an 1898 image
  are described as likely convention decoration, not assumed to be ordinary
  daily dressing. Museum of the City of New York,
  [Windsor Hotel record](https://collections.mcny.org/C.aspx?IID=2F3XC5MTMTM&PN=8&VBID=24UAYWFTVLY&VF=DamViewDetailPopup&VP3=CMS3).

The flag image is generated by the game and is not a third-party asset.

## Signs

The first pass contains hotel identity signs, street-name blades, three `TO
LET` cards, and a few small professional, society, service, and house-number
plates. Unique lettering shares one generated atlas.

- Evidence for type and visual variety: an 1898 Fifth Avenue photograph near
  56th Street records front stairs, boarded windows, a `To Let` sign, and a
  fire alarm. Museum of the City of New York,
  [Street Scenes, 1898: Fifth Ave. at 56th St.](https://collections.mcny.org/Collection/Street%20Scenes%2C%201898.%20%20Fifth%20Ave.%2C%20S.W.%20Cor.%2056th%20St.-2F3XC58HY95C.html).
- The two hotel names and Metropolitan Club name identify represented
  buildings. Exact plate design and placement are reconstructions.
- `PHYSICIAN`, `ARCHITECT`, `SOCIETY ROOMS`, and `DELIVERIES` are plausible
  period categories, not transcriptions of signs documented at those exact
  addresses.
- House numbers 12 and 16 are explicitly atmospheric and should not be used as
  historical address evidence.

## Rendering limits

The layer is capped at three cloth flags, 20 authored sign records, 26 rendered
sign faces, four draw batches, and 3,000 triangles. It creates no colliders and
casts no shadows. Flags move only in a shared vertex shader; no cloth vertices
or instance transforms are updated on the CPU each frame.
