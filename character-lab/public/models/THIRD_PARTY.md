# Character comparison assets

`comparison-mhr-lod1.glb` is generated from Meta's official Momentum Human Rig
v1.0.1 LOD1 FBX, licensed Apache-2.0. It retains float position/normal
attributes, the LOD1 topology, 126-bone skin, 45 identity morphs, and 72
facial-expression morphs. The temporary uncompressed export avoids potential
normal terracing while 117 morph-normal targets are mixed and evaluated.

Source:

- https://github.com/facebookresearch/MHR

This file is an evaluation asset, not a final styled patient. Hair, period
clothing, skin textures, and production attachment/corrective workflows remain
separate work.
