- Role here: fitted topology, UVs, MakeClothes body mask, and the lower-skirt

The game generator supplies its own materials, A-line outer skirt, restrained
waist and hem trim, and deformation treatment. The fitted source supplies the
underlying bodice, sleeves and stable seated surface.
