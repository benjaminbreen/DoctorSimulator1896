- George Byrne, printer, stops you on the walk. He heard you are a doctor. He rolls back a sleeve: a red rash, a week old.
- Daniel Weber, shipping clerk, stops you on the walk. He heard you are a doctor. He rolls back a sleeve: a red rash, a week old.
- Henry Schmidt, insurance man, stops you on the walk. He heard you are a doctor. He rolls back a sleeve: a red rash, a week old.

- **Look at it here** _(standing +1, 5 min)_ → Heat rash. You tell him so and he pumps your hand twice.
- **Bid him call at your office** → He says he will. Men who say so on the street mostly do not.
- **Beg off and walk on** _(standing -1)_ → He watches you go. So do two others.

- **Give him a dime** _(-10¢)_ → "Obliged, sir." He is already moving on, dignity intact.
- **Stop and hear him out** _(standing +1, 5 min)_ → Cold Harbor, the Wilderness, and a pension clerk who will not answer letters. He wanted the hearing more than the coin.
- **Not today** → He touches his hat again all the same.

- **Sign the renewal** _(+50¢)_ → Signed, and half a dollar for the signature. The prescription will be filled today, and every week after.
