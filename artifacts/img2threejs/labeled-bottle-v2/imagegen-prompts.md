# ImageGen prompts

## Cork

Create a seamless square 1:1 PBR source texture for the side and top surface of
a natural bottle cork stopper. Orthographic flat material scan, evenly lit, no
perspective, object silhouette, shadows, highlights, or text. Fine tan cork
cells and tiny dark pores, restrained warm brown variation, plausible
late-19th-century laboratory bottle cork, clean but lightly aged. Tile on all
four edges and remain useful at close range as base colour. Avoid baked light,
labels, borders, seams, or large cracks.

## Paper

Create a seamless square 1:1 PBR source texture for blank aged cream rag paper
suitable for a late-19th-century laboratory bottle label. Orthographic flat
material scan, evenly lit, no perspective, object silhouette, shadows, text,
borders, or handwriting. Very fine natural paper fibres, faint warm ivory
variation, restrained foxing specks and subtle wear, still highly readable.
Tile on all four edges and work beneath dynamically rendered black typography.
Avoid baked light, folds, tears, glossy highlights, frames, or large marks.
